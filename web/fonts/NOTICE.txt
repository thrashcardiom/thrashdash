Fonts bundled with ThrashDash (so dashboards look the same offline, on Windows and Linux).
All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the full texts are in ./licenses.
Jost and Barlow Condensed are Latin subsets, converted to woff2 by tools/make_fonts.py. DSEG7 Classic declares the
Reserved Font Name "DSEG", so it is converted to woff2 WITHOUT subsetting or any other change to the glyph data.
